Questions.

Does it cost anything to join as an athlete?

No. Your page is free. MarkdAthlete keeps a small platform fee from each sponsorship to keep the lights on.

What counts as race-day proof?

Photos from the race showing the sponsor's logo or text, plus your official result or bib. For cost spots like a hotel or entry, a receipt or confirmation works too.

What if the athlete doesn't race?

If there's no proof, the sponsor gets a full refund.

Can I sponsor if I'm not a business?

Yes. Fans, family and friends can chip in from $10 and put their name or a message on the spot.

What sports are on MarkdAthlete?

We started with runners — 5K to ultra — plus HYROX and triathlon. Any endurance athlete with a race on the calendar can apply.
